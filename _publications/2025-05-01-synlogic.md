We present **SynLogic**, a comprehensive logical reasoning data synthesis framework that generates diverse, verifiable reasoning data at scale, covering 35 logical reasoning tasks including Sudoku, Game of 24, Cipher, and Arrow Maze. The framework enables controllable difficulty synthesis with rule-based verification, making the data well suited for reinforcement learning training.
